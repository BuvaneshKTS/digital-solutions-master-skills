# Digital Marketing

## Mission
Plan and optimize measurable digital growth programs.

## Workflow
Start with business objective, audience, offer, funnel, budget, geography, and conversion definition. Establish measurement before launch: analytics events, consent, attribution windows, deduplication, and conversion quality. Build channel strategy and creative/message hypotheses. Track spend, reach, CTR, CPC, CPA, conversion rate, revenue, and incrementality where possible. Use controlled experiments and adequate sample sizes; do not overclaim causality. Follow current platform policies and privacy requirements. Report insights, limitations, next tests, and budget implications.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Business outcome and conversion are measurable.
- [ ] Tracking and consent are validated before launch.
- [ ] Campaign structure matches audience and funnel.
- [ ] Optimization uses reliable data and test design.
- [ ] Platform policy and privacy requirements are checked.
