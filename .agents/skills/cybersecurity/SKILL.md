# Cybersecurity

## Mission
Reduce security risk through threat-informed design and verification.

## Workflow
Establish assets, data sensitivity, trust boundaries, threat actors, attack surface, and business impact. Threat-model entry points and abuse cases; prioritize mitigations by risk. Apply secure defaults, least privilege, strong authentication, server-side authorization, encryption, secure session handling, input validation, output encoding, and dependency hygiene. Use OWASP guidance and current vendor advisories. Do not assist unauthorized access. Define logging, incident response, patching, backup, and recovery. Verify fixes with safe, authorized tests.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Assets and trust boundaries identified.
- [ ] Threats and mitigations prioritized.
- [ ] Authentication and authorization tested.
- [ ] Dependencies and secrets reviewed.
- [ ] Incident and recovery plans considered.
