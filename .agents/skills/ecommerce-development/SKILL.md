# E-commerce Development

## Mission
Deliver secure and reliable digital commerce journeys.

## Workflow
Map the purchase journey and source of truth for products, inventory, customers, orders, payments, refunds, tax, and fulfillment. Design secure checkout and avoid storing raw payment-card data. Verify provider webhooks, signatures, idempotency, refund flows, and reconciliation. Handle stock races, pricing consistency, abandoned carts, fraud controls, privacy, and accessibility. Test guest and authenticated purchases, failures, retries, partial refunds, and fulfillment exceptions. Document operational reconciliation and support paths.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Catalog, inventory, and order ownership are clear.
- [ ] Checkout and payment provider integration are secure.
- [ ] Webhook replay and duplicate processing are handled.
- [ ] Refunds, reconciliation, and fulfillment exceptions are tested.
- [ ] Privacy and accessibility are addressed.
