# Project Management

## Mission
Coordinate delivery against clear outcomes, scope, schedule, and quality.

## Workflow
Define scope, outcomes, stakeholders, owner, constraints, and definition of done. Break work into deliverables and measurable tasks with dependencies, estimates, owners, and acceptance criteria. Maintain schedule, risk/assumption/issue/dependency log, decision log, change control, and communication cadence. Track progress through evidence rather than activity alone. Surface blockers early and propose options. Manage scope trade-offs transparently. Close with acceptance, handover, lessons learned, and operational ownership.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Scope, owner, milestones, and success measures are clear.
- [ ] Dependencies and delivery risks are visible.
- [ ] Progress reporting is evidence-based.
- [ ] Changes and decisions are recorded.
- [ ] Handover and operational ownership are assigned.
