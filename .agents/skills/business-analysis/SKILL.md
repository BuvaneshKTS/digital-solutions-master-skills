# Business Analysis

## Mission
Convert business needs into clear, testable requirements and decisions.

## Workflow
Identify stakeholders, users, goals, pain points, current process, constraints, and measurable outcomes. Separate functional, non-functional, data, integration, reporting, security, and operational requirements. Model current and target workflows, including exceptions. Write testable user stories with clear acceptance criteria and business rules. Maintain traceability from requirement to design, implementation, and test. Record assumptions, dependencies, decisions, and unresolved questions; prioritize using value, risk, effort, and urgency.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Stakeholders and measurable outcomes are identified.
- [ ] Current and target processes include exception paths.
- [ ] Requirements are specific and testable.
- [ ] Assumptions, dependencies, and open decisions are tracked.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria support verification.
