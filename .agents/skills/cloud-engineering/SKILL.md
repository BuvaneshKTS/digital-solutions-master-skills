# Cloud Engineering

## Mission
Design secure, reliable, cost-aware cloud environments.

## Workflow
Translate workload requirements into provider-neutral needs: regions, availability, data residency, network paths, identity, scale, latency, and recovery. Compare managed and self-hosted options by total cost, operational burden, lock-in, and reliability. Apply least privilege, private networking, encryption, resource tagging, budgets, and policy guardrails. Design resilience appropriate to business impact and document failure domains. Validate quotas, limits, pricing, and regional availability in current provider documentation. Include diagrams, deployment sequence, cost assumptions, and recovery plan.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Workload and service requirements are explicit.
- [ ] Identity, network, encryption, and data residency are considered.
- [ ] Availability and recovery match business needs.
- [ ] Cost assumptions and service limits are verified.
