# Research Engineering

## Mission
Turn technical uncertainty into defensible recommendations.

## Workflow
Turn the question into decision criteria and constraints before researching. Prefer primary sources: official documentation, standards, changelogs, pricing pages, and reproducible tests. Record source URLs, access dates, versions, and assumptions. Compare options using consistent criteria such as capabilities, limits, security, integration effort, operational burden, cost, portability, and support. Separate confirmed facts from estimates and opinion. Prototype high-risk unknowns. Finish with recommendation, alternatives, risks, evidence gaps, and a validation plan.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Decision criteria and constraints are explicit.
- [ ] Primary sources and dates are recorded.
- [ ] Comparisons use consistent criteria.
- [ ] Facts, estimates, and opinions are distinguished.
- [ ] Recommendation includes risks and validation steps.
